03 / 11
Why Data Matters

Data is the lifeblood
of language preservation

📜 Languages Die Without Records

When the last speaker of a language passes away and no written or audio record exists, that language is gone forever — no dictionary, no grammar, no memory.

🧠 AI Needs Data to Learn

Just as a child learns language by hearing thousands of sentences, an AI model learns by processing large amounts of text and audio. No data = no model. More data = smarter model.

🌍 Every Sentence is a Piece of Culture

Memoni carries unique proverbs, expressions and ways of thinking that cannot be directly translated. Each sentence contributed preserves not just words — but identity, humour and heritage.

⚡ The Window is Closing

Fluent Memoni speakers are concentrated in older generations. Every year without action is irreplaceable knowledge lost. The time to collect data is now — while the speakers are still here.

04 / 11
The Solution

Why build a Memoni LLM?

  • Gives the language a digital presence — searchable, usable, alive
  • Enables a Memoni chatbot similar to ChatGPT in English
  • Creates educational tools for youth to reconnect with heritage
  • Produces a permanent structured record of the language
  • Powers future mobile apps — keyboards, translators, voice assistants
  • Encourages pride and daily use across diaspora communities
  • Proven path: data → model → application
  • More data = better results. Every contribution counts.
